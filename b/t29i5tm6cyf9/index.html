Block 2 demos

Weeks 5–8 · 3 sessions a week · pyramid style, in this order

  1. 1Sit-to-stand

    12 down to 1

    Feet flat, lean forward slightly, stand tall, then sit back down with control.

  2. 2Incline push-up using a chair

    12 down to 1 (lower incline)

    Hands on the chair seat, body in one line, chest to the edge.

  3. 3Glute bridges

    12 down to 1

    Drive through your heels, lift hips, lower slowly.

  4. 4Dead bug (legs only)

    12 down to 1

    Lower back pressed to the floor, lower one leg at a time slowly.

  5. 5Bird dog (legs only)

    12 down to 1

    On hands and knees, extend one leg back, hips level, back flat.

Rest: Move straight down the pyramid, rest as needed between exercises.
