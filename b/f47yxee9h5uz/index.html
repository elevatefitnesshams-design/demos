Block 3 demos

Weeks 9–12 · 3 sessions a week · circuit style, in this order

  1. 1Reverse lunges

    30 seconds

    Step back, lower the back knee towards the floor, front knee over ankle.

  2. 2Push-up with knee taps

    30 seconds

    Do a push-up, then draw one knee towards the elbow each side.

  3. 3Plank with steps in and out

    30 seconds (one fluid motion)

    From a plank, step feet in towards hands and back out, hips steady.

  4. 4Bird dog knee hover (legs only)

    30 seconds

    On hands and knees, lift knees just off the floor, hold still.

Rest: Rest 1 minute after all 4 exercises.
