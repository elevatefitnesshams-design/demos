Block 1 demos

Weeks 1–4 · 3 sessions a week · circuit style, in this order

  1. 1Wall sit

    30 seconds

    Back flat on the wall, thighs roughly parallel to the floor.

  2. 2Glute bridge isometric hold

    30 seconds

    Squeeze your glutes, hips lifted in a straight line from knees to shoulders.

  3. 3Forearm plank on knees

    30 seconds

    Elbows under shoulders, straight line from knees to head, tummy braced.

  4. 4Incline push-up using a chair

    30 seconds, as many as you can

    Hands on the chair seat, body in one line, chest to the edge.

  5. 5Sit-to-stand

    30 seconds, as many as you can

    Feet flat, lean forward slightly, stand tall, then sit back down with control.

Rest: Rest 1 minute after all 5 exercises.
