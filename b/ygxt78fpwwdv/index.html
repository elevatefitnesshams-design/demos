Block 3 demos

Weeks 9–12 · 3 sessions a week · circuit style, in this order

  1. 1Air squat pulses

    30 seconds

    Stay low in your squat and pulse up and down a few centimetres.

  2. 2Forearm plank to high plank

    30 seconds

    Press up one arm at a time, keep hips still and level.

  3. 3Diagonal lunges

    30 seconds

    Step out on a diagonal, bend the front knee, push back to start.

  4. 4Superman hold

    30 seconds

    Lying face down, lift arms, chest and legs slightly, eyes to the floor.

Rest: Rest 1 minute after all 4 exercises.
