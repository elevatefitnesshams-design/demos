Block 2 demos

Weeks 5–8 · 3 sessions a week · pyramid style, in this order

  1. 1Bodyweight squat

    5 down to 1

    Sit back like into a chair, chest up, knees tracking over toes.

  2. 2Single-leg RDL

    5 down to 1 each side

    Hinge at the hips, back flat, reach down as your back leg lifts.

  3. 3Curtsy lunges

    5 down to 1

    Step one leg behind and across, bend both knees, chest tall.

  4. 4Inchworm with 2 push-ups

    5 down to 1

    Walk hands out to a plank, do two push-ups, walk back.

  5. 5Sit-ups

    5 down to 1

    Feet planted, curl up with control, lower back down slowly.

  6. 6Leg raises

    5 down to 1

    Lower back pressed down, lift straight legs up, lower slowly.

Rest: Rest 1 minute after each completed pyramid.
