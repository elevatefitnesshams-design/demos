Block 1 demos

Weeks 1–4 · 3 sessions a week · circuit style, in this order

  1. 1Bodyweight squat

    12 reps (hold a chair if needed)

    Sit back like into a chair, chest up, knees tracking over toes.

  2. 2Push-up

    12 reps (on knees if needed)

    Body in one straight line, lower chest towards the floor, push away.

  3. 3Bird dog

    16 reps (8 each side)

    Reach opposite arm and leg long, keep hips square to the floor.

  4. 4Marching glute bridge

    16 reps (8 each side)

    Hold hips high and level as you lift one foot at a time.

  5. 5Forearm plank

    30 seconds

    Elbows under shoulders, body straight, squeeze glutes and brace your tummy.

Rest: Rest 1 minute after all 5 exercises.
