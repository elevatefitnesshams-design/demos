Block 1 demos

Weeks 1–4 · 3 sessions a week · circuit style, in this order

  1. 1Bodyweight squat

    12 reps

    Sit back like into a chair, chest up, knees tracking over toes.

  2. 2Calf raises

    12 reps

    Rise high onto your toes, pause, lower slowly. Hold support if needed.

  3. 3Single-leg glute bridge

    8 each side

    One foot planted, other leg lifted, drive hips up through the heel.

  4. 4Push-up

    10 reps

    Body in one straight line, lower chest towards the floor, push away.

  5. 5Bird dog, knee and elbow touch

    10 reps each side

    Extend opposite arm and leg, then draw elbow and knee together underneath.

  6. 6Hollow hold

    20 seconds

    Lower back pressed down, arms and legs reaching long, ribs tucked in.

Rest: Rest 1 minute after all 6 exercises.
