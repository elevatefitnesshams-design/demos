Block 3 demos

Weeks 9–12 · 3 sessions a week · circuit style, in this order

  1. 1Bodyweight squat

    12 reps (chair-assisted if needed)

    Sit back like into a chair, chest up, knees tracking over toes.

  2. 2Floor push-up on knees

    10 reps

    Hands under shoulders, straight line from knees to head, lower chest down.

  3. 3Marching glute bridge

    16 reps

    Hold hips high and level as you lift one foot at a time.

  4. 4Hollow hold

    20 seconds

    Lower back pressed down, arms and legs reaching long, ribs tucked in.

  5. 5Ankle taps

    20 reps (10 each side)

    Shoulders slightly lifted, reach side to side to tap each heel.

  6. 6Bird dog

    12 reps

    Reach opposite arm and leg long, keep hips square to the floor.

Rest: Rest 1 minute after all 6 exercises.
